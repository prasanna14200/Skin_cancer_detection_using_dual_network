# Evaluator summary of the final EG-VAN+ prototype

We independently reconstructed EG-VAN as a seven-class skin-lesion classifier with EfficientNetV2S and a modified attention-enhanced ResNet50 branch. We selected one checkpoint on a lesion-isolated HAM validation split and froze it before its final Stage 16 evaluation. A numerical overflow in one non-local attention multiplication was corrected by computing that q@k affinity in FP32 under AMP elsewhere; this was a stability fix, not a demonstrated accuracy gain.

On the full 1,014-image HAM test, accuracy was **82.35%**, macro F1 **0.6996**, and melanoma recall **56/107 (52.34%)**. On the distinct mapped PH² external follow-up, melanoma recall was **11/40 (27.5%)**. PH² had prior project use and 80 atypical nevi were excluded, so it is not untouched external validation. These results show that good aggregate internal performance did not guarantee strong melanoma sensitivity or transfer.
