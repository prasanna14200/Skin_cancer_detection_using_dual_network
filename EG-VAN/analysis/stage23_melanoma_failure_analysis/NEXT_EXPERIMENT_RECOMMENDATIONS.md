# Ranked future hypotheses; no experiment begun

The current task is diagnostic. Stage 23 remains frozen and PH2 outcomes are **evaluation evidence**, not a hyperparameter-selection set. The ranking below weighs evidentiary support, feasibility and the risk of reusing the heavily inspected HAM validation cohort. None promises higher accuracy or melanoma recall.

| Rank | Hypothesis and controlled investigation | Supporting evidence | Feasibility and decision boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — strongest immediate value, no model training** | **Technical adequacy and source-characterization study:** independently reviewed focus, exposure, obstruction and lesion visibility labels may identify whether real acquisition defects account for some melanoma errors. Use at least two blinded reviewers with adjudication and patient/lesion grouping on a **new development cohort**; assess agreement and class-adjusted error association before defining any quality gate. | HAM Phase A sharpness association and full Phase B blur sensitivity; PH2 raw proxy differences are descriptive but unvalidated. | Moderate review effort, minimal GPU. Highest causal-interpretation value without turning PH2 misses into tuning targets. A new untouched cohort is needed for gate validation. |
| **2 — plausible but high compute and validation-selection risk** | **Single fixed training-time blur-robustness intervention:** after establishing a new lesion/site-disjoint development/selection boundary, compare otherwise unchanged Stage 23 training with one preregistered blur augmentation policy applied only to HAM training images. Primary: clean-cohort MEL recall under frozen aggregate gates; secondary: prespecified synthetic-blur robustness. | The controlled 986-image Phase B study shows severe MEL recall loss under fixed blur; it does **not** demonstrate that augmentation helps or that PH2 errors arose from blur. | Approximately another full T4 training run plus an appropriately controlled comparator. Do not pick blur strength or probability from PH2 outcomes or repeatedly search the current Stage 23 validation set. Not authorized by this report. |
| **3 — external taxonomy/generalization study, no training initially** | **Prospective label-harmonization and independent cohort study:** a newly sourced, patient-linked external cohort with pathology-reviewed labels represented in the seven-class space can test whether the PH2 OTHER route reflects category mismatch or broader transfer failure. Freeze mapping, exclusions, source processing and metrics before predictions. | Fourteen of 27 PH2 MEL misses fall in OTHER; PH2 lacks five true HAM categories and excludes atypical nevi. | High data acquisition/annotation cost, low GPU cost. No PH2 remapping or reinterpretation of current results. Follow-up findings cannot substitute for an untouched cohort. |

**Recommended next action:** the blinded technical-quality/source study together with acquisition of a genuinely new patient-linked evaluation cohort. Do not start another Stage 23 training variant from these PH2 results. If a training ablation is later approved, preregister its single change and separate development/selection and confirmation data first. No threshold, checkpoint, preprocessing, sampler or loss change is made here.
