# Stage 3B interpretation limits

1. **Reused selection cohort:** HAM validation selected the Stage 23 checkpoint and has been repeatedly analyzed. Calibration, error-detection and coverage results are exploratory, not independent confirmation. Its 743 lesion IDs permit lesion-group resampling, not proven patient-group resampling.
2. **Restricted, previously used PH2 follow-up:** only 80 common NV and 40 MEL cases are included; 80 atypical nevi were excluded by the frozen protocol. Five other HAM outputs remain possible and count incorrect for mapped accuracy. Seven-class Brier/NLL/ECE on these cases are descriptive scores for a restricted truth distribution; they do not validate seven-class external calibration. PH2 was used elsewhere in this project and is not an untouched external cohort.
3. **Missing PH2 dependence information:** PH2 `IMD` image IDs are available, but verified patient/lesion grouping is not. Bootstrap intervals resample image IDs and may understate uncertainty if repeated patients exist. Forty melanoma cases, only 13 correct, make subgroup AUROC imprecise.
4. **Calibration construct:** top-label ECE with 10 fixed bins ignores per-class calibration and changes with bin design; low ECE does not imply individual risk accuracy. Multiclass Brier is an unnormalized sum and should not be compared to a binary Brier without conversion. Softmax confidence is not a calibrated clinical probability.
5. **Error detection differs from technical quality:** predictive entropy, confidence and margin describe model output ambiguity. Stage 23 Phase B showed entropy could decrease under harmful synthetic degradation. None has human adequacy labels or is a validated technical-quality detector.
6. **Confident misses and review leakage:** some HAM melanoma misses have very high confidence. The existing 80% HAM retained-coverage scenario leaves 22/37 melanoma false negatives retained. Reporting its retained accuracy does not increase full-cohort classifier accuracy. No Stage 23 scalar cutoff was independently validated or applied to PH2.
7. **Multiple exploratory metrics:** three scores and several subgroups were examined on small reused cohorts. The cluster-bootstrap intervals are descriptive percentile intervals (1,000 replicates, fixed seed), not multiplicity-adjusted or prospective performance guarantees. ECE bootstrap intervals can be biased and sparse bins unstable.

Stage 3A remains **BLOCKED**: no eligible new cohort and qualified independent reviewers have been confirmed. Its status is unaffected by these saved-score calculations. Stage 3B made no model, threshold, training, inference or original-artifact change.
