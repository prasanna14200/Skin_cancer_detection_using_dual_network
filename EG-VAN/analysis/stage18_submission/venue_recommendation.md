# Venue recommendation

**Primary target: PLOS ONE (Public Library of Science), Research Article.** Its [scope and criteria](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/criteria-for-publication) explicitly admit methodologically rigorous original research and negative/null findings across medicine and engineering. That is the best match for a transparent seven-class reconstruction whose main result is good aggregate HAM discrimination but only 52.34% HAM melanoma recall and substantially weaker mapped PH² melanoma recall (27.5%; AUC 0.5428). This is a scope judgment, not an acceptance prediction.

The manuscript must lead with its frozen selection, lesion-isolated split, complete classwise errors and qualified external follow-up. It must disclose prior PH² project use, earlier project-level HAM test access for a different model, lack of prospective clinical validation, and the recovered checkpoint's unprovable byte identity to the missing original weights. Selective FP32 q@k is a numerical correction, not an accuracy claim. PLOS's [data policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/data-availability), [code policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/materials-software-and-code-sharing) and [AI disclosure policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/ethical-publishing-practice) create material author tasks before submission. Do not assert public availability of any local file until an actual approved release exists.

**Backup 1: BMC Medical Imaging.** It has the most direct [medical-imaging scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/12880/submission-guidelines) and a scientifically valid work criterion, but the weak external melanoma result and absence of clinical validation need prominent limits. The current listed US$3,290 APC is higher than the verified IEEE Access list fee; institution-specific reductions are unknown.

**Backup 2: IEEE Access.** Its [applied engineering and negative-result scope](https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/about/) could fit the reconstruction plus documented FP16 overflow analysis. The editorial risk is that the model reconstruction is not a demonstrated performance advance over prior models. A submission must emphasize reproducible engineering and honest failure analysis, not superiority.

**Title:** retain the master title's meaning. For PLOS sentence case use “Independent EG-VAN reconstruction for seven-class skin lesion recognition: HAM10000 test and PH² follow-up.” It is descriptive, avoids clinical-validation claims, and preserves the separate follow-up label. Alternative if authors prefer less acronym density: “Seven-class skin lesion recognition with an independent EG-VAN reconstruction: internal test and external follow-up.” No title change is made to the frozen master.

**Abstract:** the current structured paragraph covers problem, method, HAM protocol/results, PH² follow-up, checkpoint provenance, and limitation; it is below PLOS's 300-word limit. PLOS advises few abbreviations; author edit can expand first uses if desired. The weaker melanoma results must remain.
