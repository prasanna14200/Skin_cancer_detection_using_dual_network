# Stage 15 validation-only diagnosis and narrower question

Stage 14 is closed as `NO_CANDIDATE_SELECTED`. The completed artifact-recovery search found no epoch-11/16/22 checkpoints, predictions, class-wise metrics, or confusion matrices. Its saved history cannot identify which non-MEL class accounts for most of the macro-F1 decline. The earlier [design-gate diagnosis](../stage15_design_gate/diagnosis.md) remains the record of that limit; no Stage 14 rerun or inference is proposed.

The new question does **not** require class-specific attribution: can a **single, less aggressive true-MEL loss multiplier** retain at least 63/107 recalled validation MEL cases while bringing macro F1 back above its unchanged floor? Stage 9 used multiplier 1.0 and recalled 62/107 MEL cases, with macro F1 `0.6709124104985493`, accuracy `0.8225152129817445`, and NV recall `0.947209653092006`. Stage 14 used 1.5630495442733532; its near-eligible epoch 22 recalled 63/107, with MEL F1 `0.5915492957746479`, accuracy `0.8154158215010142`, NV recall `0.9291101055806938`, and macro F1 `0.6464657314142509`. Only macro F1 missed the frozen floor `0.6509124104985493`, by `0.0044466790842984`. Epoch 22 remains ineligible and is not retroactively selected.

Those two historical runs do **not** establish a monotonic multiplier-response curve or prove that the multiplier caused the metric difference. Stage 9 also used a different numerical execution policy and training trajectory. The midpoint is a **prespecified, validation-independent interpolation**, not an estimated optimum or a guarantee. One new run tests this narrow hypothesis; there is no search over candidate values.

Class-wise diagnostic evidence will be preserved prospectively in the new run: every epoch will save all validation IDs, labels, predictions, probabilities, per-class TP/FP/FN, precision, recall, F1, and the 7×7 confusion matrix. This storage correction does not change training or checkpoint selection. No HAM test or PH2 data is used.
