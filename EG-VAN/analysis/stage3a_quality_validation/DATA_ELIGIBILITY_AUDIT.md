# Stage 3A data eligibility and provenance audit

**Decision: no eligible new cohort located.** This is a repository inventory, not a claim that no suitable cohort exists elsewhere. The study cannot acquire ratings until a new cohort and reviewers are documented.

| Local material | Provenance and identifiers | Prior use / leakage concern | Stage 3A eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| `data/raw/HAM10000_images_part_1/`, `part_2/`, `data/raw/images/`, processed images and `data/splits/split_leakage_aware.csv` | HAM10000 raw JPEGs; `image_id` and `lesion_id`; cleaned metadata includes diagnosis, age, sex and site but no verified patient ID. The frozen split records lesion-disjoint partitions. | Stage 23 model training/selection and validation, Phase A proxies and full Phase B degradations used this source. HAM validation errors were examined; HAM test must remain outside new development. | Useful for historical method comparison only; **not** a new untouched cohort or independent gate-validation set. |
| `data/external/ph2/` and `data/external/PH2Dataset/` | Two local representations/mirrors of the PH2 collection, not two cohorts. PH2 has 200 images, 120 included under the frozen NV/MEL mapping (80 NV, 40 MEL) and 80 atypical-nevus exclusions; `IMD` case IDs but no verified patient linkage. | PH2 was used earlier in the project and the Stage 23 qualified follow-up; outcomes and 27 MEL misses have been inspected. | **Not** independent Stage 3A development or confirmation data; no PH2-outcome-driven rubric or threshold. |
| `analysis/stage23_image_quality_final/phase_a/` | Eight numerical proxies on the 986-image validation set, no human technical-quality labels. | Exploratory class-adjusted and error analyses already inspected. | Reference for frozen proxy definitions only. |
| `analysis/stage23_image_quality_final/phase_b_full_validation_cuda/` | 986 HAM validation images under seven conditions, including synthetic perturbations of the same images. | Baseline and degradation outcomes inspected. Synthetic copies are not new independent observations. | Motivation and stress-test context only. |

**Required new cohort intake:** original images with acquisition date/source, de-identified stable image ID, lesion ID, patient ID or documented grouping limitations, licensing/consent, format/dimensions, and source-file hashes. Check against every existing HAM/PH2 image ID and, where feasible, pixel/perceptual duplicates; verify no patient/site overlap with the intended development or confirmation boundary. Record all repeats and exclusions. Define a fixed enrollment window or source-stratified sampling frame before image review. A second source or acquisition stratum is needed to estimate source-versus-quality differences; without it, those mechanisms cannot be separated. Diagnosis is not needed for blinded quality scoring but requires independently established labels and a separately governed prediction protocol for later quality-versus-error analysis.

**Reviewer audit:** the prior [Phase A gap report](../stage23_image_quality_final/phase_a/quality_validation_gap_report.md) documents no human quality ratings or two qualified reviewers. No later reviewer roster, credentials, availability commitment, adjudicator or permission to share a new cohort was found in the inspected Stage 23 quality and failure-analysis materials. Availability is therefore **unverified**, not presumed absent. The coordinator must record both reviewers and an adjudicator before release of a blinded package.

HAM and PH2 are different sources with different formats and label spaces. Their observed score differences cannot be used to define a quality defect, a new cohort, or a quality gate. Patient-level nonoverlap with PH2 cannot be proved from its available IDs. No HAM test outcome file or PH2 prediction file was opened for this eligibility decision; prior use and cohort composition came from completed audits.
