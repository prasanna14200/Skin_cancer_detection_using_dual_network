# Stage 3A: blinded technical image-quality validation protocol

**Status: prepared, not executed.** The Stage 23 classifier, split, predictions and image preprocessing remain frozen. This protocol concerns *observable technical properties of the input image*. It does not ask a reviewer to diagnose malignancy, judge clinical adequacy, or set a model accept/reject threshold.

## Rationale and prior evidence

Stage 23 Phase A measured eight raw-image characteristics on 986 HAM validation images from 743 lesions. It found an exploratory association between lower Laplacian sharpness and 37 melanoma false negatives, but no human adequacy labels or established gain from adding quality proxies to entropy. Phase B applied six fixed synthetic degradations to those 986 images; blur sharply reduced melanoma recall, while entropy did not increase consistently with degradation. The saved-record [melanoma failure analysis](../stage23_melanoma_failure_analysis/MELANOMA_FAILURE_ANALYSIS.md) found 37 HAM validation and 27 PH2 follow-up melanoma misses. None of these results establishes that natural blur caused an individual error or that a proxy is a validated quality gate. They motivate blinded annotation only; they do not determine a cutoff or eligible image selection.

## Study population and boundary

Obtain a **new, prospectively documented development cohort** of original, unaltered dermoscopic images with source/acquisition metadata and stable lesion IDs; patient IDs or a documented patient-disjoint linkage are preferred. Include all consecutive eligible images during a fixed acquisition interval, or register a source-stratified random sample and its sampling fractions *before annotation or model outcomes are seen*. Include repeat images of a lesion and retain their linkage. Record acquisition source, device if available, raw format, dimensions, license/consent, and a de-identified linkage key. Do not filter on apparent quality, diagnosis, model prediction or error. Record unreadable/missing images with reasons before analysis. Freeze and hash the manifest and image files before review.

The local HAM train/validation/test images and both local PH2 mirrors are **not new independent cohorts**. HAM validation and PH2 outcomes have already been examined. They may provide historical context, but cannot be promoted to an untouched quality-validation set. No eligible new cohort is currently documented in the repository; see [data eligibility audit](DATA_ELIGIBILITY_AUDIT.md). Any new confirmation claim requires a separate patient/source-disjoint cohort that has not informed the rubric, proxy choice, or any quality policy.

## Blinding and review operations

1. After cohort eligibility is verified, create a private linkage table from original image/lesion/patient/source IDs to opaque review tokens. Hash the table and restrict it to a coordinator who does not rate images. Preserve original pixels and a cryptographic image hash. The displayed file name and review interface show only the token and raw image at native resolution; do not display diagnosis, source metadata, Stage 23 outputs, proxy values, or another reviewer's scores.
2. Randomize **lesion blocks** with a recorded seed chosen before review; put repeat images in the same block but hide the linkage from reviewers. Independently randomize block order for each reviewer and record the seed and generated order hashes. Do not reorder cases after observing ratings. Source-specific borders or imaging style may remain visible in the pixels, so source blinding is imperfect and must be reported.
3. Recruit at least two qualified reviewers with documented credentials relevant to dermoscopic image acquisition or image technical assessment. Give both the same written [rubric](TECHNICAL_QUALITY_RUBRIC.md) and calibration examples from outside the study cohort. They rate independently and cannot view diagnoses, model outputs, error status, each other's ratings, or the proxy table.
4. Each reviewer completes one row per image in a separate copy of [the blank template](BLINDED_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.csv). Retain every original rating. A third qualified reviewer resolves material disagreement, or two reviewers reach a recorded consensus after independent scoring. Adjudicators also remain blind to diagnosis and model error. Record adjudication reason and participants separately; do not replace the original two ratings.
5. Unblind the linkage and join source, diagnosis, proxy, and frozen model-outcome information only after annotation files and the adjudication log are locked and hashed. Later model evaluation, if authorized, needs a separately frozen protocol. Stage 3A preparation performs no inference.

## Prespecified endpoints and stopping rules

Primary endpoint: agreement on the three-level **overall technical adequacy** rating. Report exact and within-one-category agreement, linearly weighted Cohen kappa for the first two reviewers, and a lesion/patient-cluster bootstrap 95% interval. If prevalence makes kappa unstable, report category prevalence and raw agreement alongside it; do not replace an unfavorable kappa post hoc. Secondary endpoints: agreement for each of the seven technical domains; prevalence of inadequate (rating 2) and borderline (rating 1); adjudication rate; missing/unassessable rate; and association of fixed numerical proxies with adjudicated ratings. The binary *technical inadequacy* endpoint, where needed, is rating 2 versus 0/1; report the full ordinal distribution as well. This definition is for analysis, **not a deployment rejection rule**.

Exclude an item from score analysis only if its source image is absent/corrupt, lacks documented provenance or linkage, or all reviewers mark it unassessable; keep it in the flow diagram with the reason. Missing individual domain ratings remain missing, never imputed as adequate. Pause review for accidental unblinding, duplicated tokens, incorrect images or a changed source hash; preserve completed ratings and document the incident before any restart. No sample-size-dependent stopping based on agreement or model outcome. Fix final enrollment and precision target before ratings begin.

The [analysis plan](QUALITY_VALIDATION_ANALYSIS_PLAN.md) fixes comparisons and uncertainty accounting. No PH2 outcome or HAM test outcome may set quality thresholds, choose proxies, or change Stage 23. If no cohort and qualified reviewers are supplied, the study remains **BLOCKED**; no human ratings are simulated.
