# Stage 3A readiness decision

**BLOCKED for execution.** Protocol, rubric, blank annotation template, cohort eligibility audit and locked analysis plan have been prepared. No human review, inference, training or threshold optimization has occurred.

## Missing prerequisites

1. A genuinely new, legally usable development image cohort with a source manifest, original image hashes, lesion linkage and preferably patient linkage. The repository currently contains used HAM and PH2 sources only; PH2's two folders are mirrors of one collection. A separate untouched confirmation cohort is required for any eventual quality-gate validation.
2. Documented availability and credentials of at least two independent technical-image reviewers, plus a third adjudicator or recorded blinded consensus procedure. Prior Phase A found no human quality labels or documented reviewer pair; no new commitment was found in the inspected materials.
3. Cohort-specific enrollment period/sampling frame, grouping/linkage and source strata, permissions, reviewer display/calibration procedure and signed frozen manifest. Without source variation, source-related and quality-related effects cannot be disentangled.

After these inputs exist, the coordinator can generate an opaque, randomized review package, collect independent ratings and lock their hashes before outcomes/proxies are joined. The review template deliberately contains **only a header**, because no eligible cases or human scores exist. No Stage 3B is authorized by this preparation.

The current defensible image-quality status remains **exploratory characterization and synthetic robustness evidence; human technical-quality validation pending**. Existing proxy/error associations and PH2 follow-up results cannot fill this gap.
