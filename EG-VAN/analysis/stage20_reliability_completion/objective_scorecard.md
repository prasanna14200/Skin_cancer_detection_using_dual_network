# Stage 20 original-objective scorecard

| Objective | Status | Exact evidence / remaining limit |
|---|---|---|
| Original EG-VAN reconstruction | **COMPLETE WITH LIMITATION** | Final recovered epoch-16 seven-class checkpoint, Stage 16 HAM results and audited architecture. It is a documented reconstruction, not identical author code or a replication of the paper's nine-class 98.20% result. Original epoch-16 weight-file byte identity is unprovable. |
| Image-quality assessment | **PARTIAL** | Eight technical proxies on processed HAM images; Stage 20 joined all 986 validation cases and described quality/error associations. No image-quality labels, prospectively validated cutoff, or functioning pre-model accept/flag gate. |
| Uncertainty estimation | **COMPLETE WITH LIMITATION** | Final-model saved probabilities evaluated with MSP, entropy and error-detection AUROC on validation, HAM, PH²; entropy selected by validation-only rule. Scores are not a clinical uncertainty guarantee. |
| Calibration | **COMPLETE WITH LIMITATION** | Final-model ECE, seven-class Brier, NLL and reliability diagrams on validation/HAM and mapped PH². No calibration fitting; PH² interpretation limited to included NV/MEL truth and prior-use cohort. |
| Selective review | **COMPLETE WITH LIMITATION** | Validation-derived entropy cutoff `0.7675495327940953` (target ≥80% validation coverage), frozen before Stage 20 HAM/PH² application. HAM 805/1,014 retained, 106/179 errors reviewed, 23/51 MEL false negatives reviewed. Retrospective/exploratory because Stage 16 results were previously visible; not clinical safety evidence. |
| Explainability | **COMPLETE WITH LIMITATION** | Frozen final EG-VAN checkpoint: eight prespecified HAM cases, saved `resnet.nonlocal3` predicted-class Grad-CAM maps, verified hashes/dimensions and qualitative visual review. Seven maps show broad background/around-region response; no lesion masks or clinical localization validation. This is one-layer visualization, not a claim about whole-model causal attention. |
| HAM held-out evaluation | **COMPLETE WITH LIMITATION** | Stage 16 final 1,014-case evaluation audited; Stage 15 selection did not use HAM test, but earlier project-level HAM test exposure exists. Stage 20 adds no inference or threshold tuning on it. |
| PH² external follow-up | **COMPLETE WITH LIMITATION** | Stage 16 audited 120 mapped cases (80 NV, 40 MEL), excluding 80 atypical nevi; PH² had prior project use and is not untouched external validation. |
| Numerical stability | **COMPLETE** | Localized FP16 nonlocal3 q@k overflow; identical selective FP32 policy used for final model, with bounded gate and finite checkpoint. No classification improvement is attributed to this policy. |

**Overall: PARTIAL.** The final classifier, saved-output uncertainty/calibration/review analysis, external follow-up and bounded qualitative final-model Grad-CAM are evidenced. The intended pre-model image-quality accept/reject gate remains unvalidated. “EG-VAN+” is defensible only as a **qualified reliability-extension prototype**, not as a fully validated clinical or quality-gated pipeline. The frozen study remains closed; no Stage 16 result, model, or threshold was revised.
