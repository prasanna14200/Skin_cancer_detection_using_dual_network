## Why validation is first

Stage 23's 82.96% accuracy is a *validation* estimate after many project-level comparisons. Stage 25 raises it to 83.16% but lowers MEL recall 70/107→63/107, showing that aggregate improvement and melanoma performance can diverge. Synthetic blur sharply lowers MEL recall, but that experiment does not establish which real images are technically inadequate or whether blur augmentation helps. Stage 23 entropy is useful for all-class error ranking yet weak for MEL misses. A new independent evaluation and real quality labels address the missing scientific conditions before another expensive accuracy search.

**No training code, evaluation runner, new experiment directory, split, threshold, model, checkpoint, or original artifact is changed by this plan.** The next action is to approve a separately preregistered independent validation protocol and identify an eligible cohort. If the user later supplies an original numeric target, assess it against a *comparable* prespecified endpoint; do not retrofit the target to existing validation outcomes.
