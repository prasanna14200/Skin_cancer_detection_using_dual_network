# Three research conditions: evidence audit

**Scope and decision (10 October 2026).** The user confirmed that the three conditions are **uncertainty estimation, image-quality assessment, and external validation**. The repository's [early project documentation](../../docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md) discusses these workstreams, but does not state them as a numbered, binding trio with acceptance thresholds. The later [objective matrix](../stage19_objective_completion/objective_matrix.md) enumerates more than three objectives. The user's clarification fixes the scope of *this audit*; it does not retroactively create numerical success gates. No confirmed accuracy target exists. All statuses below refer to the **frozen Stage 23 epoch-14 model** unless explicitly marked historical.

## Foundation and boundaries

- Stage 23 selected checkpoint: `experiments/stage23_resnet50_imagenet_init_exp1/run/best_checkpoint.pt`, SHA256 `42b018305694392ea874c1e9a4b28457adef780f7be9e740a16506404c9fc5ab`; selected validation epoch 14. Class order: `akiec,bcc,bkl,df,mel,nv,vasc` ([Stage 23 audit](../stage23_resnet50_imagenet_init_exp1/final_stage23_audit.md)).
- Frozen lesion-aware split SHA256 `f1c04c5ef5b54372c667daf0aebc29e6f24743c6c2cc094f16e2c4e679149883`; validation 986 images/743 lesions. The naive historical image split has different leakage risk. Prior HAM test and PH2 use elsewhere in the project prevents calling them wholly untouched across the entire research program.
- This audit independently streamed the local checkpoint and split bytes and reproduced both SHA256 values above. It did not deserialize the checkpoint or run inference.
- Stage 23 uncertainty and quality are **validation-only exploratory** results on the same cohort used in multiple model-selection rounds. They measure implemented methods; they do not independently validate a deployment gate.
- No training, inference, checkpoint edits, split edits, test-based selection, or PH2-based selection were performed in this audit.
