# Reviewer-style audit before manuscript revision

**Scope:** manuscript-quality control of the closed Stage 15–16 study. This directory is an editorial review, not a new ML experiment. The complete main manuscript, supplement, freeze note, Stage 16 final reports, prediction CSVs, metric JSONs, manifests, and figure files were inspected before editing.

| Area / section | Rating | Problem and importance | Evidence | Proposed correction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Title | MINOR | “Frozen” in the title reads like internal workflow language rather than a research-paper title. | Main title. | State independent reconstruction and two evaluation domains succinctly. |
| Abstract | MINOR | Correct numbers, but limited description of the external mapping and checkpoint recovery. | Main Abstract; Stage 16 metric JSON; recovery audit. | Preserve results and add a compact scope/limitation statement after body revision. |
| Introduction/background | MAJOR | Only one reference, and no related-work context for HAM10000, PH², backbones, non-local attention or focal loss. This weakens traceability and novelty framing. | Main Introduction/References; verified primary publications. | Add concise related work and verified primary citations; avoid SOTA comparisons. |
| Published-model comparison | MAJOR | The reconstruction is a seven-class HAM study; the published EG-VAN paper reports a different task. Without explicit distinction, readers could infer direct replication or benchmark comparability. | Local published paper PDF; Stage 8B architecture audit; main Methods. | State architectural reconstruction and differing class/task scope; do not compare headline metrics. |
| Dataset/split/leakage | MAJOR | Split sizes and lesion isolation are stated, but split provenance and earlier project-level test access are not explicit. “Held-out” could be read as never used in the repository. | Frozen split CSV/hash; Stage 10 archive; Stage 15 preregistration. | Give split SHA256, lesion-level separation, and clarify test was withheld from Stage 15 selection, although an earlier project stage evaluated another model. |
| Architecture/attention/fusion | MAJOR | Branch names are given, but the reader cannot see where SCGA, non-local attention, serial MFF, and terminal fusion occur; source-paper underspecification is only deferred to another audit. | Main Methods; Stage 8B architecture report and local paper. | Add a concise architecture paragraph, cite the published model and backbone/non-local primary papers, identify reconstruction assumptions. |
| Preprocessing/training | MINOR | HAM and PH² paths, sampler draws, scheduler/augmentation and loss policy need sharper separation for reproducibility. | Stage 15 config/preregistration; Stage 11 PH² protocol. | State deterministic evaluation transforms and frozen PH² pipeline; preserve exact registered scientific factors. |
| Numerical policy | PASS | FP16 q@k overflow and selective FP32 policy are distinguished from classification outcomes. | Stage 13C traces/audits; Stage 15 numerical protocol; main Methods. | Retain, with a precise statement that this changed execution precision only. |
| Checkpoint/validation selection | PASS | Frozen validation gates and eligible minimum-loss selection are described; test is excluded from selection. | Stage 15 selection rule, recovered epoch-16 validation metrics. | Retain; add exact provenance references. |

| Results story/tables/figures | PASS | Validation → HAM test → melanoma errors → PH² is coherent. Four main and four supplementary figures are numbered and linked. | Main manuscript and supplement; all eight saved figures; final tables. | Retain; check captions and source artifacts after editing. |

| Reproducibility | MINOR | Main manuscript lacks artifact/source pointer and final checkpoint/split provenance together in one place. | Stage 16 manifests/freeze note. | Add a compact reproducibility/data-provenance paragraph. |
| References/citations | MAJOR | Only EG-VAN is cited. HAM10000, PH², EfficientNetV2, ResNet, non-local attention and focal loss need verifiable primary references. | Main References; primary publication records. | Add verified references [2]–[7], cite the corresponding claims; mark any unverified extra claim [CITATION REQUIRED]. |

**Priority disposition before editing:** 0 CRITICAL, 6 MAJOR, 6 MINOR, 5 PASS grouped areas. The six major issues are background citations, published-model scope, split/test provenance, architecture explanation, limitations, and references.
