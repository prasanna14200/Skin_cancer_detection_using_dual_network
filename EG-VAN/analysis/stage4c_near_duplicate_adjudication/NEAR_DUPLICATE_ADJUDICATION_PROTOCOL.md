# Stage 4C near-duplicate adjudication protocol

## Purpose and frozen evidence

This is a **source-image leakage review**, not diagnosis, model evaluation, image-quality grading or patient matching by algorithm. The frozen Stage 4B version 1 split manifest is SHA256 `dd2ce3f2d698ff5cbf392ccc2a8889338fe4cb7b1017dcff677214eec392e649`. Its refined candidate CSV is SHA256 `66b9256855efa525c9b493fdad3b2707d125b6a0f324d3a1b1b920e91c8f2b51`. The first-pass dHash screen produced 6,192 candidate pairs, and the pre-existing second-pass pHash criterion (Hamming distance at most 8) retained **40 cross-boundary pairs**. Neither hash is an identity test. All 40 must be reviewed; no pair may be removed based on model predictions or performance.

The [preparation script](prepare_stage4c_review.py) checks Stage 4B input hashes, split membership, lesion IDs, raw-image hashes and metadata consistency. It produces 40 [uncropped, aspect-preserving PNG sheets](NEAR_DUPLICATE_CONTACT_SHEETS/) from the original raw JPEGs. Image A is always the development-training image and Image B the internal-selection image in the *internal* [pair audit CSV](NEAR_DUPLICATE_PAIR_REVIEW.csv); **do not give that mapping to blinded reviewers**. The image display is only a scaled presentation; the linked raw JPEG is the authoritative full-resolution evidence. No pixel alteration or crop is applied to the raw source file. The [evidence manifest](stage4c_review_manifest.json) records SHA256 hashes for every source and sheet.

## Review workflow

1. A coordinator assigns immutable pair IDs P001–P040. Give each reviewer only the contact sheets and an independent copy of [BLINDED_REVIEW_FORM.csv](BLINDED_REVIEW_FORM.csv). Do not show training/selection roles, model outputs, validation outcomes, PH2 outcomes, or another reviewer's decisions. The sheets contain image IDs, lesion IDs and available HAM source fields; they show no model results. Reviewers may open the linked original JPEGs at native resolution.
2. Seek at least two independent qualified reviewers with experience in dermoscopic image/source-image comparison. This study has **not** established that such reviewers are available. Reviewers must not infer malignancy or clinical diagnosis from the sheet. They may request original acquisition/source records through a data steward if available.
3. For every pair, record exactly one decision and a brief evidence-based note. Permitted decisions: **Confirmed duplicate** (same source capture or pixel-level derivative), **Same lesion / different capture**, **Likely same patient**, **Unrelated lookalike**, **Unresolved**. Record a source-record identifier or raw-image evidence path where available. Similarity distance alone cannot justify any decision. Distinct metadata may help but cannot prove patient disjointness; metadata may be incomplete or inconsistent.
4. The coordinator compares reviewer decisions. Agreement on the five labels may be summarized with raw agreement and Cohen's kappa if two reviewers complete all pairs; report the confusion table and uncertainty in the small sample. Disagreements and unresolved cases go to joint adjudication or source-record review, with original independent responses preserved. Do not edit the blank original template or fabricate a completed review.
5. Join adjudicated decisions back to the internal pair CSV by pair ID, retaining both reviewers' signed/date-stamped forms and source evidence. A conservative leakage decision is required before any split revision or training authorization. Do not use diagnosis/model accuracy to resolve a pair.

## Source fields and limitations

`HAM10000_metadata.csv` and `metadata_clean.csv` provide image ID, lesion ID, `dx`, diagnosis source (`dx_type`), age, sex and anatomic localization. They provide **no patient ID**, acquisition session, source institution key or reliable patient linkage. Across the 40 flagged pairs, 29 share `dx`, 33 share `dx_type`, 2 have identical recorded age, 19 share sex, and 4 share localization. These counts are only metadata descriptors. Age differs in 38 pairs, sex in 21, localization in 36 and diagnosis in 11; none of those differences is used as an automatic unrelated decision. The 40 pair decisions remain **blank** at preparation.

This review cannot certify that all unflagged images come from different patients. It can only address the flagged image pairs and the linked lesion groups under the stated screen. A new patient-linked cohort is still required for an independent confirmation of any future model claim.
