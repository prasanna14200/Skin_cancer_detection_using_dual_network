# Stage 4A data governance and evaluation-boundary audit

**Decision: BLOCKED for a clean new model-selection claim or replacement of frozen Stage 23.** A controlled *exploratory* de novo comparison could be registered using only original HAM training lesions, but no new locked selection partition, untouched Stage 23 comparator cohort, or independent confirmation cohort currently exists. This is a read-only audit; no training, inference, resplitting or threshold change occurred.

## Inventory and frozen identifiers

The canonical HAM image directory contains 10,015 JPEGs, one for every unique `image_id` in `data/splits/split_leakage_aware.csv` (SHA256 `f1c04c5ef5b54372c667daf0aebc29e6f24743c6c2cc094f16e2c4e679149883`). The leakage-aware split has **8,015 train images / 5,973 lesions**, **986 validation images / 743 lesions**, and **1,014 test images / 754 lesions**. It contains 7,470 distinct `lesion_id` values, no duplicate `image_id`, and no lesion crossing partitions. Classwise counts and duplicate checks are in [the leakage audit](LEAKAGE_AND_DUPLICATE_AUDIT.md). The cleaned HAM metadata has lesion IDs, diagnosis and limited demographic/site fields but **no patient ID**. Lesion isolation is proven from this CSV; patient independence is not.

`data/splits/split_naive.csv` is a second assignment of the **same** 10,015 HAM images, not a second dataset. It has 764 lesions crossing assignments and was used in earlier naive baseline work. It must not be mixed with the leakage-aware split. The flat HAM raw images and `HAM10000_images_part_1/part_2/` are source/consolidated representations, not independent cohorts.

PH2 is one 200-case secondary-mirror collection represented in local folders `data/external/ph2/` and `data/external/PH2Dataset/`. The fixed Stage 23 follow-up mapped 80 common nevi to NV and 40 melanomas to MEL, excluding 80 atypical nevi. PH2 provides case/image IDs but no verified patient linkage. It has been used for preprocessing diagnostics and older-model external evaluations before Stage 23, and again for Stage 23 follow-up. The two folders do **not** provide independent cohorts. Other public datasets mentioned in plans, including BCN20000, have not been established as downloaded, screened, non-overlapping local evaluation cohorts. Generic ImageNet/torchvision weights are initialization sources, not local HAM or PH2 partitions.

## Exposure chain

The [exposure matrix](DATASET_EXPOSURE_MATRIX.csv) records partition roles. Early EfficientNet naive and leakage-aware baselines produced saved test metrics; the older naive partition also has lesion crossing. Stage 9–15 reconstructed EG-VAN development used HAM train and validation. Stage 16 evaluated the earlier recovered Stage 15 candidate on the leakage-aware **HAM test** and PH2; Stage 20 reused these saved outcomes for reliability. Stage 22 inspected Stage 16 test errors in planning. Stage 23–25 runners used only HAM train/validation according to their audited source/manifests, and Stage 23 epoch 14 was selected on 986-case validation. Stage 24 had no eligible checkpoint; Stage 25 was compared on the same validation partition and retained Stage 23 as current candidate. Stage 23 validation was subsequently reused for uncertainty, quality Phase A/B, melanoma failure and Stage 3B analyses. PH2 was used in Stage 23 qualified follow-up and later error/uncertainty work. The Stage 15/20 entropy review cutoff was selected from older validation and tested on exposed outcomes; Stage 23 coverage scenarios are exploratory and have **no independently validated threshold**.

Preprocessing was developed as a paper-informed fixed pipeline with underspecified hair-removal/Gray World/Retinex choices logged in `docs/DEVIATIONS.md`. Training augmentation was also changed during early implementation. Stage 23–25 froze their respective preprocessing path; no evidence says Stage 23 directly tuned preprocessing on PH2, but earlier PH2 diagnostics and broad project-level exposure mean **indirect influence cannot be ruled out**. Phase B synthetic degradation did not modify preprocessing or checkpoint selection. No causal claim that a historical result directly drove a specific code change is inferred where the record does not show it.

## Can the HAM test be called untouched?

**No.** A file named `test` in the split is not a fresh lockbox. Earlier baseline runs wrote test outcomes; Stage 16 evaluated the Stage 15 candidate on this 1,014-image partition; Stage 20 and Stage 22 inspected saved results. The [frozen Stage 23 boundary audit](../final_model_freeze/evaluation_boundary_audit.md) finds no verified Stage 23 test inference and the Stage 23–25 training runners excluded test, but concludes project-level test exposure prevents an untouched Stage 23 confirmation claim. The 1,014 images can remain historical evaluation evidence; their outcomes must not choose a new experiment, checkpoint, hyperparameter, threshold or stopping time.

## What remains eligible?

Original HAM **training** lesions may be used for future gradient updates under a new, separately registered experiment. Before training, a **new internal selection boundary from those training lesions only** could be fixed for freshly initialized *control and treatment* models. Both models must be trained without seeing that selection subset. This would yield an exploratory, internally controlled comparison; it is **not** an independent test. The existing frozen Stage 23 checkpoint already trained on all original HAM training lesions, so evaluating it on a holdout carved from those lesions would be in-sample and is **not a fair control comparison**. Do not relabel the repeatedly inspected original HAM validation or test as fresh. PH2 findings remain previously exposed follow-up, unusable for development decisions. A genuinely new patient-linked, non-overlapping cohort is needed for independent confirmation of any new candidate against frozen Stage 23.

The [controlled protocol](CONTROLLED_DEVELOPMENT_PROTOCOL.md) specifies the only defensible conditional path and a rule fixed before any future run. The current readiness conclusion is [BLOCKED](STAGE4A_READINESS.md), because that boundary and confirmation cohort have not been established. No new experiment is authorized by this audit.
